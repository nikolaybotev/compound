# Review instructions

## Passes

Run three passes and tag each finding with its pass:

- **Bugs:** an answer from the ARM concepts skill that states a payment, a reset rate for the user's loan, or an index value, or a procedure that disagrees with `intent/arm-loan/spec.md` requirement 2 (a periodic cap on the first change, a periodic cap measured from the start rate, a ceiling that is not start rate plus lifetime cap); a payment, total interest, or interest-saved figure that disagrees with the fixtures in `intent/mortgage-skill/spec.md` or `intent/loan-recast/spec.md`; a schedule that keeps going after the principal is zero; an extra amount that does not reduce principal; a skill that supplies a loan amount, rate, term, or extra plan the user did not give; a recast payment that is not the second run's `monthly_payment_cents`; recast interest taken from the first run's remaining interest instead of the second run's `interest_cents`; an invented recast fee, tax, or escrow payment. On an ARM, a payment, rate, reset payment, or interest figure that disagrees with `intent/arm-loan/spec.md` requirement 18's fixtures (not the fixed-rate fixtures); a first reset governed by the periodic cap instead of the initial cap; a rate above the ceiling or below the floor in effect; a reset payment that is not the level payment on the actual balance over the remaining months; an index accepted at a month that is not a reset month; a fixed-rate JSON, CSV, or summary that changed; a page figure that disagrees with `buildReport` for the same `arm` object; a skill that supplies a fixed period, interval, margin, cap, floor, or index value the user did not give. On the amortization page, a payment, balance, payoff month, or interest-saved figure that disagrees with `buildReport` for the same loan; a second walk in the page; a start month treated as payment 1; Apply adding to the extra column instead of replacing it. On the summary, an Extra principal paid that is not `extra_applied_cents`, and a Months saved or payoff duration that does not match `months_saved` or `payoff_month`. On Saved by extra, a cell that shows the cumulative `interest_saved_cents` for that month, or a year row whose Saved by extra is not the sum of its rows (the year subtotal is a sum of cells and is not Interest saved). On the extra column, a cell marked edited that equals the applied map, and a form select that regained the native appearance. On the complete loan picture, a principal-and-interest figure that is not `monthly_payment_cents`, or a second amortization beside `buildReport` (that line means the Principal and interest row; the Prevailing principal and interest row is the modal payment). On loan scenarios, a wiped version-1 loan; a version-2 value written into the legacy key; an import that keeps a bad file's loans; a prevailing extra taken from the requested map; a one-month lump counted as the prevailing extra; a heading dollar that is not the prevailing total; a partial save treated as a newer loan; or a removed last scenario. A Pages deploy that uploads production without `prototype/`. On origination fees, a payment, amount financed, finance charge, or points figure that disagrees with `intent/origination-fees/spec.md`; a charge computed from a payment that was rounded before the discount; a skill that supplies a note rate, APR, or loan size the user did not give.
- **Security:** secrets or personal loan files committed to the repo; unexpected network or dependencies; reading or writing paths other than the script, the `--extra` file and the `--index` file the user passed, and the skill's own temporary CSVs. On the amortization page, a request to any host other than the page's own files, a font loaded from a network, or an install step on the calculator's `node --test` job.
- **Compliance:** the change matches the spec and plan for the intent it implements (`intent/mortgage-skill/`, `intent/loan-recast/`, `intent/amortization-app/`, `intent/extra-savings-column/`, `intent/complete-loan-picture/`, `intent/origination-fees/`, `intent/arm-loan/`, `intent/arm-concepts/`, `intent/loan-scenarios/`, or `intent/ui-followups/`), and the conventions in `AGENTS.md`. A departure from the plan that is not written back into `plan.md` in the same change is a compliance finding.

## What Important means here

Reserve Important for findings that report the wrong interest or the wrong interest saved, keep charging after payoff, apply an extra payment to something other than principal, invent loan terms, report the wrong recast payment, report the wrong prepaid finance charge or points, report a wrong ARM worst-case payment or rate, or ship a change the spec forbids (on an ARM, a figure the spec forbids). On the amortization page, the same bar covers a figure that disagrees with `buildReport`, a chart band that does not tie out to the loan amount or the total interest, a prefill that changes the schedule before Apply, and a changed Saved by extra month cell. Style and naming are nits.

## Cap the nits

Report at most five nits per review; summarize the rest as a count.

## Do not report

Formatting the CI already enforces, generated files the repo ignores, and wording inside the verbatim prompt block of any `intent.md`.
