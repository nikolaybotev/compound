# Review instructions

## Passes

Run three passes and tag each finding with its pass:

- **Bugs:** an even-out extra equal to the no-extra jump; a floor, first-adjustment range, even-out extra, or yearly-extra figure the skill or the page computes instead of reading from the CLI or `buildReport`; a displayed payment that is not the walk's `payment_cents`; a figure written into the schedule or the Extra Payments form; an answer from the ARM concepts skill that states a payment, a reset rate, or an index value for the user's loan, that presents a labeled illustration as the user's loan, interest-only or option-ARM arithmetic added to `amortize.js`, the calculator CLI, the mortgage-loan-calculator skill, origination fees, or the page, or a procedure that disagrees with `intent/arm-loan/spec.md` requirement 2 (a periodic cap on the first change, a periodic cap measured from the start rate, a ceiling that is not start rate plus lifetime cap); a payment, total interest, or interest-saved figure that disagrees with the fixtures in `intent/mortgage-skill/spec.md` or `intent/loan-recast/spec.md`; a schedule that keeps going after the principal is zero; an extra amount that does not reduce principal; a skill that supplies a loan amount, rate, term, or extra plan the user did not give; a recast payment that is not the second run's `monthly_payment_cents`; recast interest taken from the first run's remaining interest instead of the second run's `interest_cents`; an invented recast fee, tax, or escrow payment. On an ARM, a payment, rate, reset payment, or interest figure that disagrees with `intent/arm-loan/spec.md` requirement 18's fixtures (not the fixed-rate fixtures); a first reset governed by the periodic cap instead of the initial cap; a rate above the ceiling or below the floor in effect; a reset payment that is not the level payment on the actual balance over the remaining months; an index accepted at a month that is not a reset month; a fixed-rate JSON, CSV, or summary that changed; a page figure that disagrees with `buildReport` for the same `arm` object; a skill that supplies a fixed period, interval, margin, cap, floor, or index value the user did not give. On the amortization page, a payment, balance, payoff month, or interest-saved figure that disagrees with `buildReport` for the same loan; a second walk in the page; a start month treated as payment 1; Apply adding to the extra column instead of replacing it. On the summary, an Extra principal paid that is not `extra_applied_cents`, and a Months saved or payoff duration that does not match `months_saved` or `payoff_month`; a payoff date that is still the accrual month instead of the due date on the 1st. On the complete loan picture, a principal-and-interest figure that is not `monthly_payment_cents`, or a second amortization beside `buildReport` (that line means the Principal and interest row; the Prevailing principal and interest row is the modal payment). On Taxes and Insurance, a subtotal that is not property tax plus home insurance plus conventional PMI. On the summary prevailing bar, a component amount that is not that segment's cents, or a zero share that is drawn. A Pages deploy that uploads production without `prototype/`. On closing costs, a percentage origination fee; a Closing costs line that includes points; a clean reference total other than 1657241 cents on the fixture inputs in `intent/clean-closing-costs/spec.md` requirement 11; a 365 prepaid-interest charge that was single-rounded; or the institution's name on the page. On origination fees, a payment, amount financed, finance charge, or points figure that disagrees with `intent/origination-fees/spec.md`; a charge computed from a payment that was rounded before the discount; a skill that supplies a note rate, APR, or loan size the user did not give. On an ARM level extra, a post-reset extra other than `intent/arm-extra-level/spec.md` requirement 3, or an index commit that leaves a later period's extra unchanged or a stale positive extra in place while `appliedLevel` is true and no extra amount is edited. On Moving in, a funding date that is not the start month and closing day; a Closing Disclosure count that treats a Sunday or a 5 U.S.C. 6103(a) holiday, including the observed Friday or Monday, as a business day; a pre-filled signing date; or schedule rows relabeled for an interest credit. On the Tax table, interest paid taken from the accrual-year sum instead of prepaid interest plus rows whose due date falls in that year; a tax figure or a PMI badge on a schedule row; or the case-study loan loaded as a fresh visit. On the knowledge panel, an ARM types article that is not the Types section of the concepts skill, a Closing Disclosure, acceleration, partial-prepayment, or teaser article that is not the section in `docs/kb/`, or a term that navigates the loan page away.
- **Security:** secrets, keys, or real config committed to the repo; unexpected network or dependencies; reading or writing paths other than the script, the `--extra` file and the `--index` file the user passed, and the skill's own temporary CSVs. A departure from the plan that is not written back into `plan.md` in the same change is a compliance finding.

## What Important means here

Reserve Important for findings that report the wrong interest or the wrong interest saved, keep charging after payoff, apply an extra payment to something other than principal, invent loan terms, report the wrong recast payment, report the wrong prepaid finance charge or points, report a wrong ARM worst-case payment or rate, or ship a change the spec forbids (on an ARM, a figure the spec forbids). On the amortization page, the same bar covers a figure that disagrees with `buildReport`, a chart band that does not tie out to the loan amount or the total interest, a prefill that changes the schedule before Apply, and a changed Saved by extra month cell. Style and naming are nits.

## Cap the nits

Report at most five nits per review; summarize the rest as a count.

## Do not report

Formatting the CI already enforces, generated files the repo ignores, and wording inside the verbatim prompt block of any `intent.md`.

## Writing

Never hardcode this repo's own owner, name, or URL, including the Pages site URL where it can be derived. Prose about the repo says "this repo". Distribution entry points may hardcode the canonical location: a curl|sh one-liner, an installer's default source, or a dependency on another repo. Add an optional override for forks where it is cheap. Do not derive a repo identity from `GITHUB_REPOSITORY` in code that other repos' CI may run. Inside this repo's own workflows, `${{ github.repository }}` is fine. Do not make install instructions depend on an env var the reader must set. References to other repos are fine.

Don't copy the current value of a setting into docs, tests, or workflows. Refer to the setting by its name (env var, secret, or config key). Tests check behavior against the configured value, not a literal.

In the README and other human-facing user or setup docs, drop notes a competent reader already knows, such as "(healthy: exit 0)". `AGENTS.md`, this file, skills, and intents written for agents keep those explicit expectations.
