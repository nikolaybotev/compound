# Part D. Pricing and the rate lock

<a id="part-d"></a>

The initial Loan Estimate's three-business-day delivery rule is the [TRID citation note](../TRID_LOAN_ESTIMATE_COMPLIANCE_CITATIONS.md). This page does not restate it. The clocks below are the later ones: a lock, a revised estimate, and the Closing Disclosure.

<a id="points"></a>

## D1. Points, "par", and lender credits

- **Discount points:** an upfront charge to buy a lower rate. **1 point = 1% of the loan amount.** On the Loan Estimate they appear in Section A as "% of Loan Amount (Points)" (**Verified**, [12 CFR 1026.37(f)(1)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/subchapter-B/part-1026/subpart-C/section-1026.37)).
- **Par rate:** the rate with zero points and zero lender credits. "The rate straddles par" means the rate sheet steps from small points just below par to small credits just above it. Rate sheets are priced in 1/8-point steps.
- **Lender credit:** the reverse trade. A higher rate, and part of the closing costs is paid as a credit.
- **Origination fee vs points:** an origination fee is the charge for making the loan. It may be quoted as a percent, but it is not by itself a rate buydown. Discount points are tied to the rate. Both are prepaid finance charges in the APR, and both generally count toward qualified-mortgage points-and-fees ([C3](qualifying.md#qm)).
- **Breakeven:** cost of points ÷ monthly payment savings = months to recover the cost.

> **Illustration.** $400,000: paying 1 point ($4,000) to go from 6.50% to 6.25% saves about $65.40/month in principal and interest. Breakeven ≈ 61 months. **Inference:** on a hybrid ARM the points only buy a lower *start* rate, so they pay off only during the initial period (or until a refinance or sale). The margin, and therefore every later rate, is usually unchanged. This illustration is not a reader's loan.

<a id="rate-lock"></a>

## D2. Rate locks, float-downs, and switching programs (fixed-rate and ARM)

- **Rate lock:** a commitment to a rate and price for a set number of days. Within **three business days** after the rate is locked, a revised Loan Estimate must go out with the locked rate, points, lender credits, and other rate-dependent terms (**Verified**, [12 CFR 1026.19(e)(3)(iv)(D)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/subchapter-B/part-1026/subpart-C/section-1026.19)).
- **Float-down:** an option to lower a locked rate if the market improves. Whether one exists, how many are allowed, what it costs, and when it can be used are **Inference:** lender policy, not regulation. Get the terms in writing.
- **Switching programs** (for example from a 30-year fixed-rate loan to a 7/1 ARM) is a borrower-requested change. **Inference:** in practice it usually means:
  - new pricing from the ARM rate sheet;
  - a new or transferred lock (ask whether the existing lock date, expiration, and float-down rights carry over);
  - re-running underwriting at the ARM's qualifying rate ([C2](qualifying.md#qualifying-rate));
  - the ARM program disclosure and CHARM booklet, if not already given ([F1](documents.md#program-disclosure));
  - a **revised Loan Estimate**. Regulation Z allows revised estimates for "revisions requested by the consumer" (**Verified**, 12 CFR 1026.19(e)(3)(iv)(C)), and a revised Loan Estimate must be received no later than **four business days before consummation** (**Verified**, 12 CFR 1026.19(e)(4)(ii)).
- **After the Closing Disclosure:** changing the **loan product** after the Closing Disclosure has been provided requires a corrected Closing Disclosure and a **new three-business-day waiting period** (**Verified**, [12 CFR 1026.19(f)(2)(ii)(B)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/subchapter-B/part-1026/subpart-C/section-1026.19)). The same applies if the APR becomes inaccurate or a prepayment penalty is added. Make the fixed-rate versus ARM decision well before the Closing Disclosure.
- **Loan Estimate expiration:** if intent to proceed is not indicated within 10 business days of the Loan Estimate (or a longer period the creditor specifies), a new one may be issued (**Verified**, 12 CFR 1026.19(e)(3)(iv)(E)).
