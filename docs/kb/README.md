# ARM knowledge base

Consumer education for choosing between a 30-year fixed-rate loan and a hybrid ARM, such as a 7/1 or a 7/6. It is general education, not legal, tax, or lending advice. Every loan's own note controls.

Prepared from the ARM know-how explainer of 2026-10-23. The page opens these articles beside the loan: a side panel on a wide screen and a bottom sheet on a phone. The ARM types explainer is the Types section of the concepts skill, shown from that file.

The named adjustment procedure, the two-axis types, and the page's labeled illustrations stay in [`.agents/skills/arm-loan-concepts/SKILL.md`](../../.agents/skills/arm-loan-concepts/SKILL.md). Its design is [`intent/arm-concepts/`](../../intent/arm-concepts/). This knowledge base does not restate that procedure or those illustrations. The page's general scenario is a purchase price of $600,000 with 5% down, so the loan is $570,000. Nashua at 1.683% and Brentwood at 1.32% in that skill are local tax rates on the purchase price, not a note rate, an index, or a cap.

## Conventions

- **Verified** means checked on 2026-10-23 against the primary source linked in that section: the eCFR text of Regulation Z (title 12 up to date as of 2026-10-21), the CFPB CHARM booklet, the FFIEC/CFPB APOR methodology, the Fannie Mae Selling and Servicing Guides, and the Homeowners Protection Act (12 U.S.C. 4901–4902).
- **Inference** marks the author's reasoning, or common market practice that no rule requires. An **Inference** stays an **Inference**.
- **Illustration** marks invented example numbers. These pages name no lender. None of an illustration describes a real borrower or loan.
- Terms follow the CFPB CHARM booklet: **initial period** (the time the starting rate lasts) and **adjustment period** (the time between later rate changes). Regulation Z's own text says "initial fixed-rate period", and that wording is quoted only where a rule is quoted.
- Citation style matches [`docs/TRID_LOAN_ESTIMATE_COMPLIANCE_CITATIONS.md`](../TRID_LOAN_ESTIMATE_COMPLIANCE_CITATIONS.md): an eCFR link and the quoted rule text. Anchors are for term-label deep links.
