# Part H. Glossary

<a id="part-h"></a>

- **Acceleration:** the right to demand the full balance after default or an unapproved transfer. [F4](documents.md#acceleration)
- **Adjustment period:** (CHARM) the time between rate changes after the initial period, for example 1 year in a 7/1 or 6 months in a 7/6. [A2](arm-rate.md#initial-period)
- **Adjustment notices:** the initial notice 210–240 days before the first new payment, and later notices 60–120 days before each new payment (25–120 only for an adjustment every 60 days or more often, and for certain pre-2015 loans). [A6](arm-rate.md#notices)
- **AIR table:** the Adjustable Interest Rate table on Loan Estimate page 2 and Closing Disclosure page 4. [F2](documents.md#le-cd)
- **Amortization:** how each payment splits between interest and principal over time. The concepts skill's Types section defines positive, zero, and negative amortization. [B1](payment-structures.md#amortizing)
- **AP table:** the Adjustable Payment table (interest-only, optional, step, or seasonal payments). [B4](payment-structures.md#balloon)
- **APOR:** average prime offer rate, a weekly benchmark APR. ARMs are matched by initial period. [C4](qualifying.md#hpml)
- **APR:** annual percentage rate, the cost of credit including prepaid finance charges. It is a composite for ARMs. [C5](qualifying.md#apr)
- **ARM program disclosure:** the description of an ARM program, due early in the process. [F1](documents.md#program-disclosure)
- **Assumable:** a buyer of the home may take over the loan on its existing terms, with approval. [E4](prepaying.md#assumable)
- **ATR (ability to repay):** the federal rule that a creditor must reasonably determine the borrower can repay. [C2](qualifying.md#qualifying-rate)
- **Balloon payment:** a payment more than twice a regular periodic payment. [B4](payment-structures.md#balloon)
- **Caps (initial / periodic / lifetime):** limits on rate changes. [A3](arm-rate.md#caps)
- **Ceiling (maximum rate):** start rate + lifetime cap. [A3](arm-rate.md#caps)
- **Change date:** the date a new rate takes effect. The new payment is due on the next payment date. The payment due on the change date is still the previous amount. [A2](arm-rate.md#initial-period)
- **CHARM booklet:** the CFPB's Consumer Handbook on Adjustable-Rate Mortgages. [F1](documents.md#program-disclosure)
- **Closing Disclosure (CD):** the final federal summary, received at least 3 business days before signing. [F3](documents.md#cd-vs-note)
- **CMT (Constant Maturity Treasury):** a Treasury yield index published by the Federal Reserve (H.15). [A1](arm-rate.md#note-rate)
- **Conversion option:** a note right to switch the ARM to a fixed rate by formula. [E3](prepaying.md#conversion)
- **Discount points:** an upfront charge (1 point = 1% of the loan) for a lower rate. [D1](pricing.md#points)
- **DTI:** debt-to-income ratio. [C1](qualifying.md#dti)
- **Due-on-sale:** a clause allowing acceleration on transfer of the property. [F4](documents.md#acceleration)
- **Fallback / replacement-index clause:** how the index is replaced if it is discontinued. [A5](arm-rate.md#index-replacement)
- **Float-down:** an option to lower a locked rate. Its terms are lender policy. **Inference.** [D2](pricing.md#rate-lock)
- **Floor (minimum rate):** the lowest rate allowed. Often equal to the margin. [A3](arm-rate.md#caps)
- **Fully indexed rate:** index + margin. [A1](arm-rate.md#note-rate)
- **HPA:** the Homeowners Protection Act (PMI cancellation at 80%, termination at 78%, midpoint). [E6](prepaying.md#pmi-removal)
- **HPML / HPCT:** higher-priced mortgage loan / higher-priced covered transaction (APR at least APOR + 1.5 for first liens; HPML jumbo + 2.5). [C4](qualifying.md#hpml)
- **Index:** the published benchmark named in the note. [A1](arm-rate.md#note-rate)
- **Initial period:** (CHARM) how long the initial rate lasts, for example 7 years in a 7/1. [A2](arm-rate.md#initial-period)
- **Interest-only:** payments that cover only interest for a period. [B2](payment-structures.md#interest-only)
- **Lender credit:** a credit toward closing costs in exchange for a higher rate. [D1](pricing.md#points)
- **Lookback:** how far before the change date the index is read (often 45 days). [A2](arm-rate.md#initial-period)
- **Margin:** the fixed points added to the index. [A1](arm-rate.md#note-rate)
- **Negative amortization:** payments below the interest due, so the balance grows. [B3](payment-structures.md#negative-amortization)
- **Note rate:** the interest rate in the note currently charged. On a hybrid ARM during the initial period, it is the start rate. [A1](arm-rate.md#note-rate)
- **Par rate:** the rate with zero points and zero credits. [D1](pricing.md#points)
- **Partial payment (Closing Disclosure sense):** a payment *smaller* than the amount due. This is not a prepayment. [E2](prepaying.md#extra-principal)
- **Partial prepayment / extra principal:** paying principal early, without paying the loan off. [E2](prepaying.md#extra-principal)
- **Payment shock:** **Inference.** A plain name for the payment increase when a discounted initial rate ends or the rate rises at a change date. [A4](arm-rate.md#teaser)
- **Points and fees (qualified mortgage):** the charges counted against the qualified-mortgage limit (3% for 2026 loans of $137,958 or more). [C3](qualifying.md#qm)
- **Prepayment penalty:** a charge for paying principal early. Banned on ARMs under 12 CFR 1026.43(g). [E1](prepaying.md#prepayment)
- **Qualified mortgage (QM):** a loan meeting Regulation Z's product and price limits. [C3](qualifying.md#qm)
- **Qualifying rate:** the rate used to compute the payment for approval. [C2](qualifying.md#qualifying-rate)
- **Rate lock:** a commitment to a rate and price for a set period. A revised Loan Estimate follows within 3 business days. [D2](pricing.md#rate-lock)
- **Recast (servicer recast):** re-amortizing a fixed-rate loan's payment after a large prepayment, at the servicer's option. On an ARM, every change date re-amortizes automatically. [E2](prepaying.md#extra-principal)
- **Refinance:** a new loan replacing the old one. [E3](prepaying.md#conversion)
- **Seasoning:** time elapsed since the loan began. Used for PMI removal and seasoned qualified mortgages. [E5](prepaying.md#seasoning)
- **SOFR (30-day average):** the index Fannie Mae ARMs use today. [A1](arm-rate.md#note-rate)
- **Teaser / start / discounted rate:** a start rate below index + margin. [A4](arm-rate.md#teaser)
