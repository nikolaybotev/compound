# Part G. What to ask

<a id="part-g"></a>

Phrase every answer request as "please confirm in writing". Every question works for any creditor. These pages name no lender.

**ARM terms**

1. What are the index, margin, lookback, and rounding rule? Where is the index published?
2. What are the first, periodic, and lifetime caps? What is the floor? Do the caps apply to decreases too?
3. Is the start rate below today's index + margin (a discount)? If so, by how much?
4. Exactly which payment is the first one at a new rate? How often does the rate change after that? What is the **initial period**, and what is the **adjustment period**?
5. What does the fallback (replacement-index) clause say? Can the margin also be replaced?
6. Is there a conversion option? What are its formula, windows, and fee?
7. Is the loan assumable? On what conditions?
8. Do the note's grace period, late charge, and prepayment terms differ from the fixed-rate note?

**Qualifying and pricing**

9. What qualifying rate was used, and what was the DTI at that rate?
10. Is the loan a qualified mortgage? Is it a higher-priced mortgage loan (APR vs APOR)? Will escrow be required?
11. What are the par rate and the points or credits at each rate step? Is any charge discount points rather than an origination fee?
12. If the program changes, does the lock (date, expiration, float-down) carry over? When will the revised Loan Estimate arrive?

**Prepaying and PMI**

13. Is there any prepayment penalty? (On an ARM there cannot be one under federal rules. Ask anyway, and confirm the Loan Estimate says NO.)
14. How are principal-only payments sent so they are not treated as future installments? Is there a minimum?
15. On an ARM, will extra principal lower the payment at the next change date?
16. Who is the mortgage-insurance investor? What are the rules for removal based on original value and on current value?

**Documents**

17. Please send the ARM program disclosure and blank copies of the ARM note, the ARM rider, and the deed of trust.
