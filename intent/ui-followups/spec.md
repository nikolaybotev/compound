## 1. Summary

Ten changes to the amortization page in `apps/web`, all in one release. The start month becomes a month picker that the page draws itself, so Safari and Firefox get the same control as Chromium. The summary drops the fixed-mode extra-payment sentence, adds "Extra principal paid" from the report's `extra_applied_cents`, and prints months saved and the payoff duration in years and months. The extra-payments form lines its select up with its text fields, moves the Apply sentence beside the button with new wording, and shows cells edited since the last Apply in blue. The year rows gain a Saved by extra subtotal. The chart legend follows the pointer and never sits under it. Nothing changes in `amortize.js`, the CLI, the skills, or the workflows.

This folder builds on [intent/amortization-app/](../amortization-app/spec.md), [intent/extra-savings-column/](../extra-savings-column/spec.md), [intent/complete-loan-picture/](../complete-loan-picture/spec.md), [intent/arm-loan/](../arm-loan/spec.md), and [intent/feature-previews/](../feature-previews/spec.md). Where this spec changes a sentence of one of those, including the fixed-mode clause of `intent/arm-loan/` requirement 14, D20 names it and this spec wins. Everything else in them stands.

## 2. Goals and non-goals

**Goals**

- G1. A month picker for the start month that works the same in every browser (item 1).
- G2. A fixed-rate summary without the extra-payment sentence (item 2), with "Extra principal paid" (item 3), and with months saved and payoff duration readable in years and months (items 4 and 5).
- G3. An extra-payments form whose select lines up with its text fields in Safari (item 6), whose Apply sentence sits beside the button and says what Apply does (item 7), and whose edited cells are blue until Apply (item 8).
- G4. A Saved by extra subtotal on each year row (item 9).
- G5. A chart legend that follows the pointer and leaves the bars under the pointer reachable, on a desktop and on a phone (item 10).

**Non-goals**

- Any change to `amortize.js`, `compound_interest_monthly.js`, `origination_fees.js`, `amortize.d.ts`, the two skills, or `node --test`.
- Any change to the ARM rate math, the ARM terms, the Index cell, or the ARM summary lines. The ARM payment note keeps its words.
- Any change to the deploy workflows, the `gh-pages` branch, `PAGES_POINT_VERSION`, the storage key scheme, or the CodeQL work on its own branch. Bumping the app's point version is the owner's release step, not part of this intent.
- A new npm dependency for the picker or the legend. The page has `preact` and nothing else at runtime.
- A typeable start-month text field, a day picker, or a date range.
- Changing what Saved by extra means on a month row, or making the summary's Interest saved the sum of the column.
- Changing the `Months saved` or `Payoff date` region names, or the `monthly_payment_cents` that the heading and the picture show.

**Release phasing.** Every requirement below is this release. Nothing is tagged later.

## 3. Principles

- P1. One walk. Every new number is read from the `buildReport` result the page already has (`extra_applied_cents`, `months_saved`, `payoff_month`) or is a sum of cells the page already computes (the Saved by extra subtotal). No new call pattern and no second formula.
- P2. One control in every browser. The start month is drawn by the page, so a Safari visit and a Chromium visit see the same picker, and Playwright on Chromium exercises the control the owner uses.
- P3. Words follow the product. The fixed-rate note goes; the ARM note stays because the payment really does reset.
- P4. Blue means "this cell differs from what Apply last wrote." It is a per-month comparison against the applied map, not a dirty flag, so a cell edited back to the applied amount is no longer blue, and a cell cleared to empty is blue by its border. Apply itself still replaces the whole map, blue cells and black ones alike.
- P5. The legend is a tooltip. It is placed relative to the pointer, it never captures the pointer, and it may leave the chart's box to stay on screen.
- P6. The year subtotal is a sum of the cells in that year and nothing more. It is not the plan's savings, and the spec says so where a builder would otherwise "fix" it.

## 4. Users and scenarios

The example loan is purchase price $570,000, 0% down, 30 years, 7%, start month October 2026, as in the earlier page specs.

- The owner opens the page in Safari. Start month reads `October 2026`. Clicking it opens a card with `◀ 2026 ▶` and twelve month buttons; `Oct` is marked as the current one. Clicking `Nov` sets the start month to November 2026 and closes the card. The first schedule row is now December 2026.
- With no extras, the summary reads Monthly payment `$3,792.22` with no sentence under it, and Payoff date `October 2056 (30 years)`. There is no Extra principal paid, Interest saved, or Months saved.
- The owner applies $100 a month. The summary shows Extra principal paid `$33,200.00`, Interest saved `$76,366.09`, Months saved `2 years 4 months`, and Payoff date `June 2054 (27 years 8 months)`. The 2026 year row's Saved by extra reads `$1,167.36`.
- The owner types `250` into month 3's extra cell and leaves it. That cell turns blue. Month 2 stays black. Clicking Apply puts `$100.00` back in month 3 and the cell is black again.
- The owner, in Safari, opens Make extra payments. The Month of year select's silver bottom border is on the same line as the two amount fields' borders, and `March` sits on their baseline. Beside the Apply button the sentence reads `Apply rewrites all extra-payment column values.`
- The owner, on a phone with the chart scrolled into view, taps a bar. The legend opens below the finger and inside the screen, not over the bar that was tapped, because there is room below. On a desktop, moving the pointer across the chart moves the card with it, to the right of the pointer until the card would leave the window, then to the left. Leaving the chart hides the card unless it was pinned, as today.
- The owner switches the product to ARM. The Monthly payment note reads `Initial payment. The extra payment is on top of this amount, and the payment resets at each adjustment.` as before. Everything else in this spec applies to the ARM schedule and summary the same way.

## 5. Functional requirements

1. **Start month control (item 1).** The `<input id="start" type="month">` is replaced by a page-drawn picker. The trigger is `<button id="start" type="button">` under the existing `<label for="start">Start month</label>`, styled like the other inputs (mono type, silver bottom border, no button chrome), showing the start month as the long date the summary already uses (`October 2026`) and carrying `data-value="YYYY-MM"`. It has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`. Activating it opens a card directly below it, `role="dialog"` with `aria-label="Choose start month"`, containing: a year row with a `Previous year` button, the year as text, and a `Next year` button; then a 3×4 grid of twelve buttons whose visible text is the short month (`Jan` … `Dec`) and whose accessible name is the long month and the shown year (`October 2026`). The button for the current start month has `aria-pressed="true"`; the others `aria-pressed="false"`. The card opens on the start month's year. The year buttons change only the shown year, within `1000`–`9999`, the range the stored `YYYY-MM` grammar already allows; at a bound the button is disabled. Choosing a month commits `YYYY-MM` through the same `update("start", …)` path the input used, closes the card, and returns focus to the trigger. Escape closes the card without a change and returns focus to the trigger. Activating the trigger while the card is open closes it, and focus stays on the trigger. A pointer down outside the card, the trigger, and the trigger's `<label for="start">` closes the card without a change and leaves focus wherever that pointer down puts it. A pointer down on the trigger or on its label is not an outside pointer down: the click that follows toggles the card, so a label click opens a closed card and closes an open one, the same as a click on the trigger. Tab moves through the card's buttons in order; the picker does not add arrow-key navigation inside the grid. The picker never produces an invalid value, so the `Start month must be a month and a year.` message can only come from storage, where `parseLoan` keeps it. The stored draft stays the `YYYY-MM` string. The payment-date rule is unchanged: payment 1 is the month after the start month.

2. **Monthly payment note (item 2).** In fixed mode the `<p class="note">` under Monthly payment is not rendered. The sentence `The extra payment is on top of this amount.` appears nowhere on the fixed-rate page. In ARM mode the note is rendered with exactly `Initial payment. The extra payment is on top of this amount, and the payment resets at each adjustment.`, as `intent/arm-loan/` requirement 14 says.

3. **Extra principal paid (item 3).** When the requested-extra map has any positive amount, the summary `<dl>` gains a region named `Extra principal paid` whose value is the report's `extra_applied_cents` through `formatMoney`. It is placed immediately before `Interest saved`, so the order after `Payoff date` is: `Highest rate` and `Highest payment` (ARM only), `Extra principal paid`, `Interest saved`, `Months saved`. When the map is empty it is absent, like the other two. It is the applied total, not the requested total: on the example loan with $100 on every month the loan pays off in month 332, the figure is `$33,200.00`, and the $2,800 requested for months 333–360 is not in it.

4. **Years and months (items 4 and 5).** A formatter `yearsAndMonths(months)` in `loan.ts` turns a count of months into text: with `X = floor(months / 12)` and `Y = months % 12`, the years part `{X} year` or `{X} years` is present when `X > 0`; the months part `{Y} month` or `{Y} months` is present when `Y > 0` or when `X = 0`; the two parts are joined by one space. So 7 → `7 months`, 12 → `1 year`, 13 → `1 year 1 month`, 28 → `2 years 4 months`, 332 → `27 years 8 months`, 336 → `28 years`, 360 → `30 years`, 0 → `0 months`. This reads the items' template `X years Y months` as "print the parts that are not zero, and never print `0 years`"; a zero months part is dropped on purpose (`28 years`, not `28 years 0 months`), and F8 is the contract (D6).
   - **Months saved.** The region's value is the plain count (`7`, `0`, `11`) when `months_saved < 12`, as today, and `yearsAndMonths(months_saved)` when it is 12 or more: 28 → `2 years 4 months`, 24 → `2 years`, 179 → `14 years 11 months`.
   - **Payoff date.** The region's value is `{longDate} ({yearsAndMonths(payoff_month)})`: `October 2056 (30 years)`, `June 2054 (27 years 8 months)`, `October 2054 (28 years)`, `August 2056 (29 years 10 months)`. The count is `payoff_month`, the number of payments, which is also the number of months from the start month to the payoff month.

5. **Select alignment (item 6).** Inside `.field`, a `<select>` gets `appearance: none` (and `-webkit-appearance: none`), `border-radius: 0`, `font: inherit` resolved to the same `line-height` as the text inputs, the same `font-size` (`1.05rem`) and vertical padding (`0.35rem 0`) as `input`, right padding for a chevron, `height: auto`, `background: transparent`, and a chevron drawn as an inline SVG data URI in the background (no file, no network). The result is one box model in every engine: the select's bottom border and the adjacent input's bottom border are on the same line and their heights match. The heading's `select.product` is excluded from this rule and keeps its dotted underline and native arrow. The select's typeface stays the sans; the ask is the border and the baseline, not the font.

6. **Apply sentence (item 7).** The paragraph `Apply replaces the extra-payment column.` at the top of the extra-payments panel is removed. The Apply button and a note sit in one row (`.apply-row`, flex, baseline-aligned, wrapping on a narrow window): the button, then `<p class="note">Apply rewrites all extra-payment column values.</p>`. That sentence appears exactly once on the page. The Apply error line stays below the row.

7. **Edited cells are blue (item 8).** The scenario gains an applied map: the requested-extra map as it stood after the last Apply. Apply sets it to the map it writes (an empty Apply sets it to an empty map). A fresh visit has an empty applied map. A month's extra cell is edited when the committed extras map and the applied map disagree for that month: one has a positive amount the other does not, or the amounts differ. An edited cell's `<input class="extra">` carries `data-edited="true"` and is drawn with the new `--edited` color (`#1d4ed8`) for its text and its bottom border, so a cell cleared to empty still shows as edited by its border. A cell that is not edited carries no `data-edited` attribute and keeps the normal colors. After Apply no cell is edited. Editing a cell back to the applied amount makes it not edited. An uncommitted keystroke does not change the attribute; commit does. A term change that drops months beyond the new term drops them from both maps. The Index cell is not affected.

8. **Storage for the applied map.** The saved object stays version `1` and gains `applied: [[month, dollars], …]`, written on every save in the same shape as `extras`. On load, a missing `applied` key, or one that does not parse the way `extras` must, is treated as equal to the loaded `extras` and the loan, extras, prefill, picture, and ARM terms are kept, so a scenario saved before this release shows no blue cell until the owner edits one. A payload that already fails to load today (not JSON, wrong version, an unparsable loan or `extras`) still becomes the fresh scenario, and the fresh scenario's applied map is empty, like its extras. Feature-preview storage keys are unchanged.

9. **Saved by extra subtotal (item 9).** `ScheduleYear` gains `savedByExtraCents`, the sum of its rows' `savedByExtraCents`. The year row's Saved by extra cell shows it through `formatMoney`, `$0.00` when the year has no extra, in both the eight-column fixed table and the eleven-column ARM table. This replaces `intent/extra-savings-column/` requirement 6 and D4, which left that cell empty. The month cells and the summary's Interest saved are unchanged. The subtotals do not add up to Interest saved and are not meant to: on the example loan with $100 in months 1–12 the 2026 row reads `$1,390.60`, the 2027 row `$6,681.28`, every later row `$0.00`, their sum is `$8,071.88`, and Interest saved is `$8,137.70`. A builder who finds that gap has found the definition, not a bug.

10. **Legend placement (item 10).** The legend card is `position: fixed`, `pointer-events: none`, and placed from an anchor point in viewport coordinates with its own measured width `w` and height `h`, a gap of 16px, and a window margin of 8px:
    - The anchor is the pointer's `clientX`, `clientY` for a mouse move and for a touch tap. For the keyboard (ArrowLeft/ArrowRight, and the first focus) it is the indicated bar's center x and the chart's top edge.
    - Horizontal: `left = ax + 16` if `ax + 16 + w ≤ vw − 8`; otherwise `left = ax − 16 − w` if that is `≥ 8`; otherwise `left` is clamped into `[8, vw − w − 8]`.
    - Vertical: `top = ay + 16` if `ay + 16 + h ≤ vh − 8`; otherwise `top = ay − 16 − h` if that is `≥ 8`; otherwise `top` is clamped into `[8, vh − h − 8]`.
    - The card's width stays `min(348, chartWidth − 16)`. It may extend outside the chart's box. It never contains the anchor point when either side fits on the axis in question; when neither side fits on an axis, the clamp on that axis may put the card over the anchor, and that is the formula working, not a defect. On a 1280×720 desktop window and on a 375×667 phone window, each with the chart scrolled to the top of the viewport, the preferred side fits for the checks in AC9 and AC10. Checked 2026-10-10 against the current layout: at 1280 wide the chart is 22rem tall, a hover at 72% of its height is about 253px below its top, and the card is about 320px tall, so the card fits below the pointer only when the chart's top is within about 130px of the viewport's top; a chart that is merely in view, lower on the screen, flips the card above the pointer, which is the formula working.
    - Because the card does not take pointer events, the chart's pointer-move handler no longer skips moves whose target is inside the legend, and the month under the pointer is always the indicated month. Pinning on touch, closing on a pointer down outside the chart, arrow-key movement, and hiding the card when the pointer leaves the chart unless it is pinned keep today's behavior. The icons on the indicated bar are unchanged.

11. **What does not change.** The heading, the product select, the ARM terms disclosure, the Index cell, the picture disclosure, the chart bands, `monthly_payment_cents` in the heading and the picture, `savedByExtraCents` on a month row, `loanReport`, `groupByYear`'s slicing at `payoff_month`, the `extras` storage shape, the storage key, `node --test`, and every CLI output. The web CI job (`pnpm --dir apps/web test` and `pnpm --dir apps/web test:e2e`) must cover every acceptance criterion below except the Safari check, the production check, and the docs check.

## 7. Design decisions

**D1 — A page-drawn month picker in every browser, not a native fallback.** Checked 2026-10-10 against MDN: `<input type="month">` has usable implementations only in Chromium on the desktop and in mobile browsers; Safari and Firefox on the desktop fall back to a text field, which is the `2026-11` in the screenshot. Showing the native control where it exists and a page-drawn one elsewhere was rejected: the owner's browser would get the fallback, Playwright on Chromium would never exercise it, and two controls would need two sets of checks. One control everywhere costs the iOS wheel picker, and buys a control the suite tests. No dependency is added; the card is a `role="dialog"` with native buttons, closed on Escape and outside pointer down the way the chart card already closes.

**D2 — A button that shows the long date.** The trigger is a `<button id="start">`, so the existing `<label for="start">` keeps labelling it and keyboard users open it with Enter or Space. It shows `October 2026`, the form the summary already uses for dates, and carries `data-value="2026-10"` so a test and a reader can see the stored value. A text field that also accepted typing was rejected: the typed grammar would need validation and messages the picker makes unnecessary, and it was the text field the owner asked to replace.

**D3 — A year stepper and a twelve-button grid.** The card is the smallest control that is still a calendar: one year visible at a time, twelve months, previous and next. The month buttons' accessible names include the year, so `October 2026` names one button. Arrow-key navigation inside the grid is left out; Tab through twelve buttons is acceptable for a once-per-scenario control. The bounds `1000`–`9999` are the stored grammar's, not a product limit.

**D4 — Remove the fixed-mode note and keep the ARM note.** The owner decided this and asked for the split to be recorded. In fixed mode the sentence told the reader that the extra payment is added to the payment, which the schedule already shows and which is noise when there is no extra payment. In ARM mode the sentence is doing a second job: it says the figure is the initial payment and that the payment resets. That sentence is `intent/arm-loan/` requirement 14 and does not change. The `<p class="note">` element is not rendered in fixed mode rather than rendered empty, so nothing reserves space under the payment.

**D5 — Extra principal paid is `extra_applied_cents`.** The report carries both the applied and the unapplied extra. "Paid" is the applied amount: the dollars that actually reduced principal. The requested total would count $2,800 on F2 that was never paid because the loan was gone. The field already exists in both the fixed and the ARM report (P1). It is shown only when the map has extras, like Interest saved and Months saved, because `$0.00` under "Extra principal paid" would read as a statement about a plan that does not exist. The place is immediately before Interest saved, as item 3 says.

**D6 — One formatter for both durations.** Items 4 and 5 describe the same text, so one `yearsAndMonths` produces it. The months part is dropped when it is zero and there is a years part, because `28 years 0 months` reads worse than `28 years`, and the item's rule "the years part shows only when X > 0" is about not printing `0 years`, not about always printing the months. Singular forms are used for 1. Months saved keeps the bare count under twelve, exactly as item 4 says (`when X > 0`), and because the label already says "Months"; the payoff duration always has the text because it follows a date in parentheses. The review of Draft 1 noted that the items' literal template would print `1 year 0 months`; the owner agreed to keep F8, so the dropped zero months part is the decision, not an oversight. Checked 2026-10-10: 28 months is `2 years 4 months`. The loan-summary screenshot was taken with the November 2026 start shown in `screenshots/start-month.png`, and from November 2026 the April 2056 payoff is payment 353, `29 years 5 months`; from an October 2026 start, payment 353 is March 2056 and April 2056 is payment 354 (`29 years 6 months`). F1, F3, and AC8 are the date fixtures a builder should use.

**D7 — `appearance: none` on the form select, and the same line box as the inputs.** Safari's native `menulist-button` appearance sets its own height and ignores padding, which is why the border and baseline sit low in the screenshot. The page's `button, input { font: inherit }` rule also leaves `select` out, so the select has the UA `line-height: normal` while the inputs inherit `1.45`. Both are fixed: `appearance: none`, `font: inherit` with the input's size and padding, `border-radius: 0`, and a background-image chevron so the control still looks like a select. The heading's `select.product` is excluded because its dotted underline is its affordance and the item is about the form. The typeface stays the sans; changing the month names to mono was not asked for. The baseline cannot be read from Playwright, so AC7 checks the box model and AC12 is a Safari screenshot.

**D8 — The sentence moves next to the button and gets the owner's words.** `Apply rewrites all extra-payment column values.` is item 7's wording with the subject it needs when it stands beside the button. It replaces `intent/amortization-app/` requirement 15's "says that Apply replaces the extra-payment column". The behavior is unchanged: Apply still replaces the map.

**D9 — Edited means different from the applied map.** The owner wants to see which cells the next Apply will overwrite. A dirty flag set on commit would mark a cell that the owner changed and then changed back, and would not say anything about what Apply will do. Comparing the committed map with the map Apply last wrote does. The comparison is per month: present in one, or different amounts. Since the schedule stops at payoff, an edited month past the payoff is not visible, which is also true of the extra itself.

**D10 — The applied map is stored.** Without it a reload would turn every cell black, and the owner would lose the reason they opened the page. It is written on every save as `applied`, like `extras`, inside version 1. A scenario saved before this release has no `applied` key; treating that as "equal to extras" means the release does not paint an existing table blue, and the first hand edit after the release is the first blue cell. A corrupt `applied` is treated the same way rather than discarding the loan, because the loan is the owner's work and the applied map is a view of it.

**D11 — Blue is `#1d4ed8`, on the text and the border.** The owner chose blue. `#1d4ed8` is far from the page's red (`--interest`) and navy (`--balance`) and has contrast on the paper. The bottom border is colored as well as the text so a cell cleared to empty, which has no text, still shows as edited. The hook is `data-edited="true"` so the test reads the state and the CSS reads the same attribute.

**D12 — The year row sums Saved by extra.** `intent/extra-savings-column/` D4 left the cell blank because the subtotal is not the plan's savings. The owner has now asked for the subtotal, so the cell is the sum of the year's cells, and requirement 9 here and the `AGENTS.md` sentence that requirement 12 adds both say that it will not add up to Interest saved. The sum is of the cells the page already computes; no `buildReport` call is added. `$0.00` for a year with no extra matches the year row's other money cells.

**D13 — The legend is a fixed-position tooltip beside the pointer.** The screenshot shows the card covering the left half of a small chart. A card anchored to the chart's top with a sideways slide cannot fit beside the pointer on a phone and cannot leave the chart's box, so it covers bars. Anchoring to the pointer with a 16px gap and flipping sides when the window edge is near is the ordinary tooltip rule. `position: fixed` lets the card leave the chart's box (the chart is only 16rem tall on a narrow window; the card is taller) and keeps the arithmetic in viewport coordinates, which is also what `clientX`/`clientY` give. `pointer-events: none` is what makes the bars under the card reachable and removes the special case in the move handler. The width rule is unchanged so the card still fits a 375px window.

**D14 — Keyboard and touch anchors.** A key press has no pointer. The anchor for ArrowLeft/ArrowRight and the first focus is the indicated bar's center at the chart's top edge, so the card appears 16px under the top edge beside the bar, close to where it is today. A touch tap has coordinates, so it uses them; the card then lands below the finger when there is room, which is the phone case in the item.

**D15 — Years-and-months, Extra principal paid, and the subtotal apply to ARM mode too.** None of them depends on the product. `extra_applied_cents`, `months_saved`, and `payoff_month` are in both reports, and the ARM table's year row has a Saved by extra cell in the same column. Checked 2026-10-10 on the Example Credit Union ARM (F6).

**D16 — No change to `amortize.js`, the CLI, or the skills.** Every new figure is already in the report or is a sum of page cells (P1). Adding `extra_applied_cents` to the CLI summary or a duration to `--json` would change outputs that `intent/arm-loan/` AC1 pins byte for byte.

**D17 — Four pull requests.** The ten items touch four areas that do not share code: the summary text (items 2–5), the extra-payments form and column (items 6–9), the chart (item 10), and the start month (item 1). Each is one phase and one pull request so a review reads one area at a time. The start-month phase is last because it rewrites the `#start` step in every Playwright spec.

**D18 — Blue, not a dirty flag, is also what Apply clears.** Apply writes the applied map equal to the new extras map, so by definition nothing is edited after it. There is no second "clear colors" step and no state that could drift.

**D19 — `Months saved` under twelve stays a number.** Item 4 says the years-and-months text appears when `X > 0`. Below twelve months the region keeps today's bare count, and the existing checks for `7`, `2`, and `0` keep passing. `28` becomes `2 years 4 months` and that check changes.

**D20 — What this supersedes.** Only these points of the earlier page specs, and only for the page: `intent/amortization-app/` requirement 3 (the start month "control is a month input" → the picker in requirement 1), requirement 6 (the line under Monthly payment → removed in fixed mode, requirement 2; Payoff date gains the duration and Months saved the text, requirement 4), requirement 10 (the card "drawn over the chart" → placed by requirement 10 here, which may leave the chart), requirement 13 (the year row → gains the Saved by extra subtotal), and requirement 15 ("says that Apply replaces the extra-payment column" → requirement 6's sentence beside the button); `intent/extra-savings-column/` requirement 6 and D4 (the empty year cell → the subtotal); `intent/complete-loan-picture/` requirement 8's "The note under the summary payment stays" (→ removed in fixed mode); and one clause of `intent/arm-loan/` requirement 14, "in fixed mode it keeps today's 'The extra payment is on top of this amount.'" (→ no note in fixed mode, requirement 2). The rest of `intent/arm-loan/` requirement 14 stands, including the ARM sentence word for word, and so do its columns and its Index cell; this spec's additions sit beside them. A builder reading `intent/arm-loan/` AC9 and AC10, or the two `apps/web/e2e/arm.spec.ts` assertions that the fixed-mode region contains the old sentence, should follow this spec's requirement 2 and AC2: the sentence is gone from fixed mode and those two assertions change.
