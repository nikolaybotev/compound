## Phase 0 — Library

Files: `amortize.js`, `amortize.d.ts`, `compound_interest_monthly.test.js`

1. Add `first_adjustment`, `even_out_cents`, and `monthly_extra_cents` inside `buildReport` only when `arm.adjustmentStats` is present. An ARM report without it has none of those fields and does not run the solves. Leave the six-argument signature as it is. `{}` is allowed and means no yearly extra. A missing `yearlyExtraCents` on a present object means the same. `validateArm` ignores that object and does not treat it as an index or a cap. A present `yearlyExtraCents` that is not a safe non-negative integer (negative, fractional, `NaN`, or a string) throws an `Error` that names `yearlyExtraCents`. A positive `yearlyExtraCents` with `yearlyMonth` or `startMonth` missing, or outside 1 through 12, throws an `Error` that names that field. `yearlyExtraCents` 0 ignores the two months and does not throw, including when either month is missing or outside 1 through 12. Do not import or call a Node API.
2. Implement spec requirements 2 through 5 by walking. The worst-case probe uses an empty extra map and an empty index. The minimum probe uses an empty extra map and a present index entry of `0` at `fixedMonths + 1` only, so `resetRate` treats `0` as an index. The even-out search, for each month from 1 through `fixedMonths`, converts the candidate cents once with `Number(`${whole}.${two digits}`)`. A month is omitted only when that cent amount is zero. The comparison is one `dollarsToCents` of the walk row's dollar `payment` (that value is `payment_cents`) against `monthly_payment_cents` plus the candidate. An absent reset compares as 0. Return the smallest cent at which that payment is at or below the initial payment plus the candidate, or 0 when the no-extra payment already passes. The yearly search uses that same comparison. The yearly cents are not added on that side. For each month from 1 through the fixed period, the cent sum is `E`, plus `yearlyExtraCents` when the calendar month equals `yearlyMonth`. Convert that sum once with the same string conversion, and store those dollars. Omit the month only when the cent sum is zero. F4 keeps the January months when `E` is 0 and the yearly extra is 2000000 cents. When the yearly month never falls inside the fixed period, the map has no yearly entry and `monthly_extra_cents` equals `even_out_cents`. When `adjustmentStats` is `{}` or `yearlyExtraCents` is 0, `monthly_extra_cents` equals `even_out_cents`. Payment `m` uses `((startMonth + m - 1) % 12) + 1`, and the caller passes the start month rather than the first payment month. Do not read `extrasByMonth` or `indexByMonth` for these fields, and do not write the probe onto the schedule.
3. Tests assert F1 through F10 with `adjustmentStats` present (`{}` on the no-yearly fixtures). Include F1's second walk with $100 in months 1 through 12 and index 4.42 at month 85: `even_out_cents` stays 82321 and `first_adjustment.max_payment_cents` stays 503771. On the F1 terms with the margin at 20%, both ends are 10.875% and 503771 cents. F5 stays 7.875% and 400385 cents, with `even_out_cents` 34852. That is the no-index walk at month `F + 1`, not a re-amortization of that month at the lifetime ceiling. F6's minimum stays 3% and 255926 cents. An ARM report with no `adjustmentStats` has none of the three fields, including the existing ARM shape tests that do not pass it. `{}` on the F1 terms reports `monthly_extra_cents` 82321 and `even_out_cents` 82321. A positive `yearlyExtraCents` with `yearlyMonth` 0 and a valid `startMonth` throws, and the message names `yearlyMonth`. A positive `yearlyExtraCents` with `startMonth` 13 and a valid `yearlyMonth` throws, and the message names `startMonth`. A positive amount with `yearlyMonth` omitted, and one with `startMonth` omitted, each throw and name that field. `yearlyExtraCents` of `-1`, `1.5`, `NaN`, and `"250"` each throw and the message names `yearlyExtraCents`. `yearlyExtraCents` 0 with `yearlyMonth` 0 and `startMonth` 13 does not throw, and on the F1 terms `monthly_extra_cents` stays 82321. A fixed period of 6 months, start month 10, and `yearlyMonth` 8 leaves `monthly_extra_cents` equal to `even_out_cents`, because August does not fall in payments 1 through 6. Assert the three fixed-rate hashes and that a fixed-rate JSON has no `arm` key.
